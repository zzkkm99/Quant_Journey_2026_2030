# Strategy Research

Each strategy study should document:

- Research question and economic motivation
- Data source, frequency, date range, and cleaning rules
- Signal and position definitions
- Execution timing and protection against look-ahead bias
- Transaction costs and benchmark
- Performance and risk metrics
- In-sample/out-of-sample design
- Limitations and reproducibility instructions

The current first study is [`First_Strategy_Research.ipynb`](First_Strategy_Research.ipynb).
It is a baseline moving-average experiment, not evidence of a deployable
trading strategy.
