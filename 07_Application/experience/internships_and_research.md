# Experience Evidence

Record experience only after it has happened.

For each internship, research assistantship, or competition, record:

- Organization or supervisor.
- Dates.
- Technical responsibilities.
- Data, models, and tools used.
- Measurable deliverable.
- Public or private evidence that can be described honestly.

Avoid vague entries such as “worked on quantitative finance.” Describe the
specific model, dataset, software, and result.
